Refresh and review

Public-source refresh is an explicit administrator action outside the browser. The documented refresh command fetches four fixed primary sources, at most once per 24 hours. It stages source hashes and a candidate catalog. It sends no local evidence or inventory. Rule logic is not generated from advisory text and nothing is activated automatically.
