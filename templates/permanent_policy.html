The first confirmed native block starts a baseline. Each non-overlapping 30-day period starting 30 days later can earn one qualifying offense milestone. Three consecutive periods earn a permanent block for this exact IP. A gap resets the run; late events use their native time. Ordinary visits, source activity alone, generic evidence, counter refreshes, rolling reports, AI assessments and Palo drops do not earn milestones. Supported native offenses require saved source qualification and event provenance. Whitelists and verified crawlers take precedence.
